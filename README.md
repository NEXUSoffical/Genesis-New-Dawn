# 🌍 Genesis: New Dawn (Forever Expanding World)

> **A real-time, persistent autonomous AI simulation where civilization evolves from the primeval wilderness into a thriving city with a living dynamic economy.**

---

## 📖 Overview

In **Genesis: New Dawn**, founders **Adam and Eve** wake in a completely untouched, procedurally generated natural paradise with zero preset knowledge (**Tabula Rasa**). Driven purely by biological needs, genuine curiosity, emotional bonds, and emergent AI decision-making, they learn to gather wild sustenance, discover fire, invent tools, construct communal dwellings, farm crops, domesticate wildlife, and pass down knowledge across generations.

Players take on the role of the **Watcher**—an omniscient observer observing a living digital terrarium that runs in real-time, day and night, through the changing of four distinct seasons.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Systems

### 🧠 Tabula Rasa Emergent AI
- **No Preset Tech:** Pioneers start with completely empty minds. Fire, axes, agriculture, pottery, and masonry are discovered organically through experimentation and curiosity.
- **Dynamic Needs Hierarchy:** Hunger, warmth, energy, physical stamina, social drive, and intellectual curiosity govern all autonomous behaviors.
- **Inner Monologue & Speech:** Pioneers narrate their thoughts and vocalize feelings in real-time with speech bubbles.
- **Romance, Marriage & Dynasty:** Pioneers form emotional bonds, court each other, marry, give birth to new generations, and pass on ancestral knowledge.

### ➕ Strict 1-Character Single Spawn Rule
- **Watcher's Boon:** The player can name and spawn **exactly 1 pioneer** into the simulation.
- **Pure Simulation:** Only the name is chosen—gender (50/50), physical constitution, appearance, traits, initial vocation, starter tools, personality, and spawn location are **100% random and simulated**.
- **Permanent Lock:** Once that single character is brought to life, all spawn buttons and controls are permanently eradicated.

### ❄️ Seasons & River Hydrology
- **Four Distinct Seasons:** Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter with realistic diurnal temperature shifts.
- **Hydrology & Waterwheels:** Flowing rivers with automated riverbank waterwheels, wooden footbridges, spearfishing, and fish traps.
- **Winter Survival:** Pioneers craft winter cloaks, weave warm blankets, and gather around hearths during freezing winter cold.

### 🐎 Age of Discovery (5 Historical Pillars)
1. **Equine Domestication & Logistics:** Wild horses, stables, braided lassos, horse riding (2.2x speed), cargo carts (80kg carrying capacity), and cobblestone road speed boosts.
2. **Nautical Shipbuilding & Maritime Trade:** Wooden boats, docks, and high-speed water navigation.
3. **Culture, Libraries & Solstice Festivals:** Great Libraries, parchment scrolls, Summer & Winter Solstice dance festivals, procedural bone flutes, and tribal drums.
4. **Fortifications & Defense:** Timber palisades, watchtower gates, wooden shields, hunting bows, and sentinel horn calls.
5. **Aqueducts & Hydrology:** Stone aqueducts transporting fresh water into centralized public cisterns.

### ⏳ The Watcher's Gaze (48-Hour Unattended Rule)
- The simulation honors natural mortality: if unattended for **48 hours (2 days)** without the Watcher checking in, the founders pass away peacefully of natural causes and are honored with **Ancestral Memorial Cairns**.
- The next generation can be awakened to rebuild upon their ancestors' sacred grounds.

### ⚖️ Living Market Economy & Barter
- Natural progression from pure barter to minted currency.
- Supply and demand dynamically dictate commodity pricing (wood, fish, grain, cut stone, mud bricks, and coins).

### 💾 Persistent Civilization
- Seamless local auto-saving every 15 seconds.
- JSON save/load export to back up or restore entire civilizations.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (v18 or newer recommended)
- [npm](https://www.npmjs.com/)

### Installation & Run

```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone <repository-url>
cd genesis-world

# Install dependencies
npm install

# Start the local development server
npm run dev
```

Open your browser and visit:
```
http://127.0.0.1:5173/
```

### Controls & Navigation
- **Left-Click & Drag / Arrow Keys / WASD:** Pan camera across the boundless earth.
- **Mouse Wheel:** Zoom in and out.
- **Spacebar / Speed Controls:** Pause, 1x (True Real-Time), 5x, 20x, or 60x speed.
- **'C' Key:** Toggle immersive Cinematic Terrarium mode.
- **Minimap Radar:** Click or drag on the radar minimap in the bottom right to jump anywhere.
- **Citizen Inspector:** Click on any pioneer pill to track their needs, thoughts, relationships, and inventory.
